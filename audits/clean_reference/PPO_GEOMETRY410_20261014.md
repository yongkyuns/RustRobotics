## Main result

Removing both first-moment mixing and coordinatewise second-moment scaling from the actor step does NOT rescue the same ordinary and difficult recovery losses. All three alternatives give small positive mean-return changes relative to original Adam on both exposed panels, but no completion rescue. This rejects these particular direction replacements as a local remedy, not every optimizer effect throughout training.

The original step improves its selected minibatch loss but worsens two frozen whole-rollout surrogate objectives. The gradient-only ADAPTIVE alternative improves both rollout surrogates yet still loses the same recovery cases. Thus internal fitting consistency and physical recovery retention remain separate requirements in this witness. Neither surrogate is a true-return oracle.

This is the historical stock-SB3 reference transaction60/global Adam step130940/update410 in the RustRobotics investigation, not a new production Rust optimizer measurement. All cases are exposed development evidence.
