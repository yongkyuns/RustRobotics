# PPO 2048: recovery versus sustained balancing — October 7, 2026

**Completed development diagnosis. No new training, optimizer transactions, default changes or merge.**

## Result

The four frozen value-scaled 2048-step endpoint policies already sustain balancing for five minutes on every development case that survived the original short test. The remaining six endpoint failures occur during initial recovery. A fixed, previously published LQR controller recovers all six under paired initial conditions and noise, without losing another case.

This localizes an observable weakness; it does not identify which learning mechanism caused it or reverse the negative 8192 qualification. Four exposed development training histories are not hundreds of independent trained agents.

## Comparator contract

The earlier critic-conditioning report's six-decimal gain is used literally:

`K=[-12.326826,-18.432033,148.100494,61.271130]`.

Compute `-K*noisy_observation` in float64, divide by20, cast to float32, and use the existing normalized-force clipping contract. Stochastic cases add each paired current PPO endpoint's learned Gaussian sigma times the same per-key normal innovations. No gain tuning. All384 LQR initial physical states/noisy observations match PPO. Native disturbance/actuation/observation noise remains present. All384 LQR cases survive30000 ticks: six PPO failures recovered, zero PPO successes lost. This is a comparator, not a teacher or installed fallback, and not claimed to use byte-identical arithmetic to the earlier LQR implementation.

**ABI caveat:** `force` is clipped commanded force, not disturbed applied force. The plant adds noise/disturbances internally afterward. Do not interpret the command's20N bound as an applied-force bound.

## Disposition

The immediate observed gap is **acquiring and preserving recovery competence at difficult outward-moving starts**, not extending already-settled endpoint control from15 to300 seconds. Investigate recovery coverage, same-state action credit and actor-update effects before another training intervention. Do not substitute LQR labels/fallbacks, sweep n_steps, or tune on the now-exposed held-out cohort.

Finite frozen-policy development evidence does not qualify from-scratch reliability, all training updates, indefinite stability or hardware. No production/default/master/protected-source/deployment changes or merge. Issues#33/#35 remain open.
